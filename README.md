# Amplify | Agentes Inteligentes

Amplify is building a multi-tenant platform for configuring, training, operating, monitoring, and evolving AI agents for sales and customer operations.

Our goal is to help companies move beyond isolated AI demonstrations and operate agents with the reliability, governance, and visibility required in real business environments.

## What we're building

- Guided configuration of AI agents in natural language
- Versioned prompts, controlled publishing, and release management
- Knowledge from uploaded documents and real-time web search
- Integrations with communication channels and external business systems
- Observability for conversations, tool calls, failures, latency, and outcomes
- Multi-tenant access control, entitlements, and operational governance

## Engineering principles

We design Amplify around clear system boundaries, traceable decisions, secure tenant isolation, controlled external effects, and production-ready operations.

The platform is currently under active development. Most core repositories remain private while the architecture and first production version are being built.
